I’m a PhD candidate in the Computer Vision Group at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en), advised by [Prof. Jürgen Gall](https://pages.iai.uni-bonn.de/gall_juergen/). My research focuses on **multimodal AI and video understanding**, with an emphasis on **temporal reasoning, generative modeling, and efficient AI**. My work spans multimodal LLMs, reinforcement learning, video generation, temporal action segmentation, and long-term video anticipation.

I’ve been a Research Intern at **Microsoft Research** and **Toyota Motor Europe**, where I worked on multimodal LLMs for video perception and reasoning, as well as video understanding for manufacturing applications.

Before my PhD, I was a researcher at [Deep MI](https://deep-mi.org/), working on semantic segmentation of human brain MRI, and a visiting researcher in Prof. Gall’s group, working on action recognition and future frame prediction. I received my bachelor’s degree from the University of Tehran.
