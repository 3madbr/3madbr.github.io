I'm a PhD candidate in the Computer Vision Group at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en), advised by [Prof. Jürgen Gall](https://pages.iai.uni-bonn.de/gall_juergen/). My research is on **video understanding**, with a current focus on improving **temporal modeling in multimodal LLMs**. My earlier work covers temporal action segmentation, long-term dense anticipation, and video prediction.

I've been a Research Intern at **Toyota Motor Europe** and at **Microsoft**, where I worked on multimodal LLMs for video perception and reasoning.

Before my PhD, I was a researcher at [Deep MI](https://deep-mi.org/), working on semantic segmentation of human brain MRI, and a visiting researcher in Prof. Gall's group, working on action recognition and future frame prediction. I received my bachelor's degree from the University of Tehran.
