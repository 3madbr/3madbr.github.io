About

I’m a PhD candidate in the Computer Vision Group at the University of Bonn, advised by Prof. Jürgen Gall. My research focuses on multimodal AI and video understanding, with an emphasis on temporal reasoning, generative modeling, and efficient AI. My work spans multimodal LLMs, reinforcement learning, video generation, temporal action segmentation, and long-term video anticipation. I’m currently working on generative world models and visual-physical reasoning.

I’ve been a Research Intern at Microsoft Research, working on RL post-training of multimodal LLMs (GRPO) for video question answering and spatiotemporal reasoning, and at Toyota Motor Europe, working on video understanding for manufacturing applications.

Before my PhD, I was a researcher at Deep MI, working on semantic segmentation of human brain MRI, and a visiting researcher in Prof. Gall’s group, working on action recognition and future frame prediction. I received my bachelor’s degree from the University of Tehran.
